Vetology

Book of Laws — Path of Exile 2 Edition

Let it be known to all who roll, that the ancient rite of the Veto shall govern the selection of builds henceforth and in perpetuity. No player shall be compelled to suffer the indignity of a build they did not choose, nor shall any player be exempt from the wrath of another's Veto.

Article I
The Roll

Each player shall roll for their build as they have always rolled, by whatever means the company has seen fit to provide. The roll proceeds without modification. Let fate speak first before the Council of Vetoes is convened.

Article II
The Allotment of Vetoes

Upon completion of the roll, each player shall receive a total of ten (10) Vetoes, distributed thusly: five (5) Self-Vetoes, used to reroll one's own result, and five (5) Hostile Vetoes, wielded against another player's settled result.

Article III
The Settled State

A player is considered Settled when they have declared satisfaction with their current roll. No Hostile Veto may be cast against an Unsettled player. The Veto is a tool of disruption, not of acceleration.

Article IV
The Right of Reprisal

Should a player who is Settled and holds exactly one (1) remaining Veto of any kind be struck by a Hostile Veto, they shall immediately be granted one (1) additional Veto of Reprisal. This Veto may only be directed at another player, never at oneself.

⚠ Clarification

The Reprisal Veto is granted once per incident. Furthermore, it cannot chain. A player who receives a Reprisal Veto does not trigger Reprisal if they themselves are then Vetoed while holding only one Veto. The cycle of vengeance must end.

Article V
Exhaustion of Vetoes

A player who has expended all ten of their Vetoes must accept their current roll as final, regardless of dissatisfaction. They may appeal to the goodwill of fellow players, but they have no legal recourse under this Book of Laws. They made their choices.
